How it works

  1. 1

    Report it

    Lost or found something? Fill in your name, email, phone and the item details: name, category, colour, description, where and when.

  2. 2

    Finders hand it in

    If you found an item, give it to the Lost & Found administrator. Nobody contacts the owner directly.

  3. 3

    Admin matches

    The administrator compares lost and found reports (with help from smart match suggestions) and links the right pair.

  4. 4

    Verified handover

    Only if the details match, the administrator contacts the owner and hands the item over. Owner and finder never see each other's details.
