About FindIt Campus

FindIt Campus is a lost & found service built for one community: Mehran University of Engineering and Technology. Students and staff lose wallets, phones, keys, ID cards and bags every week, and the people who find them rarely know who to return them to. FindIt Campus puts both sides in one place and uses simple, transparent matching to bring them together.

Collecting an item safely

  1. Report your lost item with your name, email, phone and the item details. The administrator will contact you if a matching item is handed in.
  2. Or send a claim on a found item's page with details only the owner would know. Never post those details publicly.
  3. Wait for verification. An administrator compares your proof with what the finder reported. You can check progress with your claim code on the claim status page.
  4. Meet in a public, staffed place such as the lost & found office or a security desk. Avoid isolated spots, and bring a friend if you prefer.
  5. Bring photo ID (student card or national ID). The person handing over the item should check it.
  6. Check your belongings before leaving, and tell an administrator if anything is missing.
Ownership is always verified before handover. A match score is only a suggestion. Nothing is approved automatically, and no item is released without an administrator confirming the owner.

Found something?

Post a found-item report, then hand the item to the lost & found administrator. Owner and finder never contact each other directly; the administrator connects them. Don't publish serial numbers, card numbers or other identifying details in the public description. Keep one detail back so the real owner can prove it's theirs.

Your privacy

Your name, email and phone number are visible only to campus administrators. Claim proofs are never shown publicly. Reports can be hidden or removed by an administrator if they are inappropriate or duplicated.

Contact & help

Lost something valuable such as a passport or bank card? Please also report it to the relevant authority or your bank straight away.
